1. Introduction & Motivation

🚨 Road Safety Statistics

  • Over 3,500 fatalities annually in India caused by road potholes and asphalt degradation.
  • Massive vehicular repair expenses from wheel and suspension damage.
  • Monsoon weather accelerates road surface cavitation.

💡 Vision-Based Solution

  • Real-time automated detection using standard dashcams or phone cameras.
  • Eliminates slow, high-cost manual inspection routines.
  • Immediate driver alerts + structured maintenance data for municipal agencies.

2. Problem Statement & Objectives

Goal: Design an end-to-end computer vision system to detect potholes, quantify surface severity, and deliver proactive road safety advisories.

Construct classical OpenCV spatial-domain pipeline (Canny, morphology, contours).

2. Deep Learning

3. Proposed 3-Layer System Architecture

Layer 1: Classical Baseline

  • Grayscale Conversion
  • Gaussian Noise Filtering

Layer 2: Deep Learning (YOLOv8)

Layer 3: Road Safety Logic

  • Area Ratio = Box Area / Image Area
  • Low (<2%) | Med (2-6%) | High (>6%)
  • Pothole Counter & Damage Rate
  • Driver Caution Alert Banners

4. Classical Image Processing Pipeline

Pipeline Stages

  1. Grayscale: Standard luminosity formula removes color bandwidth.
  2. Gaussian Smoothing: 5x5 kernel suppresses asphalt surface grain.
  3. Canny Edge Extraction: Sobel gradient with dual threshold hysteresis.
  4. Morphological Closing: Elliptical kernel seals broken edge loops.
  5. Contour Filtering: Filters blobs by area and aspect ratio.

Observations

  • Fast execution (~10 ms per frame on CPU).
  • Requires zero training or annotated datasets.
  • Limitation: Vulnerable to false positives from tree shadows and road markings.

5. YOLOv8 Deep Learning Detector

Training Setup

  • Dataset: 665 annotated road images (70% train / 20% val / 10% test).
  • Platform: Google Colab Tesla T4 GPU (~32 minutes).
  • Epochs: 50 epochs, batch size 16, image resolution 640x640.
  • Weights: Exported as models/best.pt (22.5 MB).

Quantitative Results

  • mAP@0.50: 81.4%
  • mAP@0.50:0.95: 52.8%
  • Precision: 84.2%
  • Recall: 78.6%
  • Latency: ~4.2 ms (GPU) / ~31 ms (CPU)

6. Severity Assessment & Warning Logic

Formula: Severity Ratio = (Bounding Box Area) / (Frame Area)

Low Severity (<2%)

Shallow cavity or minor surface distress. Advisory warning issued.

Medium Severity (2% - 6%)

Moderate road depression. Speed caution and safe braking advisory.

High Severity (>6%)

Large hazard capable of vehicle damage. Critical alert triggered!
